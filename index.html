About CouponItch

CouponItch is an independent coupon discovery platform for apps, games, software, subscriptions and everyday shopping. Our aim is to make interesting offers easy to find in one simple place.

Good finds, picked by people

Listings are added and maintained manually. We bring offers together to make browsing easier; we do not automatically scan, test or verify coupons.

Some listings link to general brand pages. Check each destination for current offers; a listing does not guarantee a discount. Brands and product names belong to their respective owners; their appearance does not imply endorsement or a commercial relationship.

Privacy Policy

This site does not require an account and does not include analytics, advertising trackers, marketing cookies or embedded third-party content.

Data stored in your browser

The site uses localStorage for the optional developer testing mode and its timer preference. First-party cookies store a randomized availability count and update schedule for each promo for up to 30 days. These counts are simulations saved in your browser, not live merchant stock or real redemption data. They are not used for advertising or cross-site tracking. If cookies are blocked, the counters work only until you leave or reload the page. Search queries and category selections remain in page memory and are not sent to us. You can clear stored preferences through your browser’s site-data settings.

Log handling and retention depend on the hosting service’s configuration. External websites have their own privacy policies and may use cookies or tracking technologies when you follow a link.
